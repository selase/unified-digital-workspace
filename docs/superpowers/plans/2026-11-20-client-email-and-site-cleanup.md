# Client email setup and Thyroid site cleanup (20 Nov 2026)

## Decisions
- Mailboxes stay with Google Workspace or Zoho. The platform never hosts mailboxes and never stores a client's mail password.
- Platform mail is sent through SES. Each client verifies its own domain with SES, so site mail can be sent as `forms@` or `info@` that domain.
- DNS is generated by the platform. It is applied automatically only when the platform holds the Route 53 zone, and never overwrites a record that already exists unless forced.
- A custom MAIL FROM subdomain (`bounce.<domain>`) keeps the client's root SPF record for the mailbox provider alone, so Google and SES do not clash.

## Site cleanup
- One form per page: Membership, Donate and Volunteer show their own form and no second enquiry form. The generic enquiry form stays only where it is the page's single form (Partner With Us, and Donate/Membership fallbacks).
- Contact is details only (phone, email, address, map). The "general" enquiry type was removed.

## Out of scope for now
- Admin screen for the Email tab, SNS bounce and complaint webhooks, send limits, Domain Connect.
