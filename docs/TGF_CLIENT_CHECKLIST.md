# Thyroid Ghana Foundation website: what we need from you

Your website now has photo banners, a Contact page and an Events page. The items below are the ones only the foundation can provide. They are listed in order of how much they improve trust and results. Send what you have, even if it is not perfect, and we will place it on the site.

Tick each item as you send it.

## 0. Email for the foundation (most urgent)

- [ ] **Subscribe to Google Workspace** for thyroidghanafoundation.org (check Google for Nonprofits first), with one mailbox, info@thyroidghanafoundation.org. Choose the password yourselves and do not send it to us. Tell us when it is created and we add the technical settings to the domain.
- [ ] Tell us whether anyone reads info@ today.

## 1. Trust and credibility

- [ ] **Registration details:** NGO registration number and the year the foundation was founded.
- [ ] **Who oversees the money:** one or two sentences on governance (board approval, audit, or who signs off spending).
- [ ] **Annual or financial report:** even a one-page summary of income, spending and programmes (PDF is fine).
- [ ] **Real figures, with the year:** people screened, screening camps held, surgeries subsidised, regions reached. The home page and the Our Impact page must show the same numbers.
- [ ] **Partners and sponsors:** a logo file and one line for each (for example MDS-Lancet Laboratories, University of Ghana Medical Centre). We will add a partners strip to the Partner With Us page.
- [ ] **Office hours** and a **short Google Maps link** to the office (the address and phone are already on the Contact page).

## 2. Health information (to be checked by your doctors)

- [ ] **A named medical reviewer** and the "last reviewed" date for the Thyroid Disease and Do I Have Thyroid Disease pages.
- [ ] **Where to get care:** a list of clinics, endocrinologists and screening sites in Ghana, with town and phone number. Patients ask for this most.
- [ ] **Short pages (a few paragraphs each) on:** symptoms, treatment options, diet, thyroid disease in pregnancy, and thyroid disease in children.
- [ ] **Practical FAQ answers:** what a test costs, where to get tested, and whether the foundation can give medical advice.
- [ ] **Patient stories:** two or three short testimonies, with each person's written permission and a photo if they are happy to share one.

## 3. Giving and membership

- [ ] **Paystack account details** so the site can take donations and membership fees online. Until then the Donate page shows your contact details and an enquiry form instead of a gift form.
- [ ] **What a gift pays for**, for example "GHS 100 funds one screening". Please give three or four amounts with the cost behind each.
- [ ] **Bank and mobile money details**, if the foundation wants them shown on the Donate page for people who prefer to give that way.
- [ ] **Who receives the alert** when someone applies for membership, so a staff email address can be set up.

## 4. Events and news

- [ ] **Your next events:** title, date, place, a two-line description and a sign-up link if there is one. We will enter them, or show your team how to add them in the website admin.
- [ ] **Recent news and photos** from events in the last year.
- [ ] **Resources to download:** leaflets, posters and event reports (PDF).

## 5. Legal documents (from the foundation or its lawyer)

We do not write legal text for you. Please send:

- [ ] **Privacy Policy**
- [ ] **Terms of Use**
- [ ] **Medical Disclaimer** (the website's health information does not replace a doctor's advice)
- [ ] Whether the foundation is registered with the **Data Protection Commission** of Ghana, and the registration number if so.

Once we have the final text we publish each page and link it from the website footer.

## 6. Accounts we need access to

- [ ] **Google Search Console** access, so we can submit the sitemap and watch for problems.
- [ ] **Facebook and YouTube** pages: confirm the links in the footer are the correct, current ones. Tell us if the foundation is on Instagram, X or LinkedIn.
- [ ] **Newsletter tool** (for example Mailchimp), if the foundation wants a sign-up box on the site.
